How to Treat Leaf Spot

Leaf spot can be treated by first removing and discarding all affected leaves to stop the spread of the disease. Make sure to avoid watering the leaves directly—water only the soil, as moisture on leaves encourages fungal and bacterial growth. Place the plant in an area with good air circulation and bright, indirect light, and avoid overcrowding nearby plants. If the problem continues, apply a fungicide or bactericide suitable for indoor plants, following the instructions carefully. Also, ensure proper drainage in the pot and avoid overwatering, as consistently wet soil weakens the plant and makes leaf spot worse. Regularly cleaning tools and pots helps prevent future infections.
